Every skill here was written by us, for our own setup, and then generalized for this repo. Where an idea came from someone else's public work, we say so. We left out every skill we installed from other people, even ones we had edited.

## Left out on purpose

Skills we use but did not write (design, video, writing-process and security packs from other authors, course material, and official vendor skills) are not here, whatever their license. If you want them, get them from their authors.
